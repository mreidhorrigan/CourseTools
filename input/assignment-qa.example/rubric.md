# Interface analysis rubric — 10 points

## Claim — 3 points

- 3: A specific, contestable claim explains how the interface organizes action.
- 2: A relevant claim is present but broad or mainly descriptive.
- 1: A topic is present without a clear claim.
- 0: No relevant claim.

## Evidence — 4 points

- 4: Two accurately cited interface observations directly support the claim.
- 3: Two observations are present, with a minor citation or connection problem.
- 2: One useful observation supports the claim.
- 1: Evidence is vague or unsupported.
- 0: No relevant evidence.

## Limitations — 2 points

- 2: A distinct concluding paragraph identifies a meaningful limit of the analysis.
- 1: A limitation is mentioned but remains vague.
- 0: No limitation.

## Format — 1 point

- 1: The submission is 600–800 words and uses Markdown or DOCX.
- 0: Either requirement is unmet.
