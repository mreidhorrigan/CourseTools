# Research backlog: interactive digital narrative pathway

Investigate whether the five-week videogame-design round should offer an interactive digital narrative (IDN) pathway instead of, or alongside, the current procedural-ecosystem pathway.

The review should compare:

- alignment with IAT 210 learning outcomes and the 4-5-4 course sequence;
- whether one shared rubric can assess both pathways fairly or separate criteria are required;
- teachable IDN structures, including branching, stateful, parser-based, spatial, and emergent narrative;
- accessible and low-cost authoring environments suitable for an asynchronous course;
- scope, technical prerequisites, team roles, version control, playtesting, and submission packaging;
- common instruction, examples, readings, terminology, and assessment evidence shared with procedural ecosystem;
- workload parity and the risk that a nominal choice produces substantially different technical burdens;
- quiz and examination coverage if students follow different production pathways; and
- whether the strongest design is a choice of pathways, a required hybrid, or a later replacement.

Do not change the current Fall 2026 assignment until the pathways, workload, rubric validity, accessibility, and assessment consequences have been reviewed together.
