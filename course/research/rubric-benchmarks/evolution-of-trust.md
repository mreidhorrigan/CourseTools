## Design evidence

The player repeatedly chooses to cooperate or cheat in an iterated trust game. Immediate payoffs make each choice legible. The work then converts the same interaction into tournaments among strategies such as Copycat, Always Cheat, Always Cooperate, Grudger, and Detective. A population model reproduces successful strategies, creating an evolutionary feedback loop.

Later interactive sections expose environmental parameters. Players alter population composition, payoff values, number of repeated rounds, and the probability of mistakes or miscommunication. Visible outcomes change reproducibly in response. These controls demonstrate that cooperation is an emergent property of interacting rules and conditions rather than a scripted ending.

The experience progressively introduces controls, explains every strategy, provides immediate visual feedback, and includes reset paths. Text and controls make the operative model inspectable. The public repository, source history, translation instructions, credits, and license inventory provide unusually strong technical and documentation evidence for a browser work.
