# Existing-game evidence dossier: Root

Benchmark purpose: test whether the Board-Game Final rubric can recognize exemplary, already-published design work. This is evidence about an existing game, not a student submission.

## Identity and inspectable package

- Title: *Root: A Game of Woodland Might and Right*, designed by Cole Wehrle and published by Leder Games.
- Official resource library: https://ledergames.com/pages/resources
- Official September 5, 2024 rules reference: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0106/0162/7706/files/Root_Base_Law_of_Root_Sep_5_2024.pdf?v=1729176570
- The resource library also publishes a graphical Learning to Play guide, walkthrough, faction materials, variants, and maintained rules for independent use.

## Design evidence

Root is a complete physical strategy game with a shared woodland map, cards, faction boards, warriors, buildings, tokens, items, dice, and markers. The Law of Root provides strict definitions, precedence rules, component limits, setup, victory conditions, actions, faction procedures, variants, a glossary, and an index. A separate Learning to Play guide supplies conversational instruction and graphical examples.

Players pursue victory through sharply asymmetric factions operating in one shared ecosystem. The Marquise builds an engine and controls territory; the Eyrie programs an expanding decree that can collapse into turmoil; the Woodland Alliance converts sympathy and supporters into revolt; the Vagabond develops relationships, items, and mobility. Shared clearings, card suits, crafting, movement, battle, and scoring make each faction’s actions alter the opportunities and threats faced by every other faction. Non-binding negotiation adds another feedback channel.

The design creates consequential choices, visible board-state feedback, multiple strategic paths, and self-balancing political incentives. Precise terminology and explicit conflict-resolution rules support independent play. Leder Games maintains dated rulebooks, clarifications, learning materials, replacement support, digital implementations, and variant material, demonstrating sustained testing and revision.

## Applicability limit

Publicly available rules do not constitute a printable substitute for every commercial component, a hypothetical IAT 210 student contribution statement, or a course playtest dossier. Grade those course-submission requirements only in the separate full-rubric result. The portable design result excludes “Individual contribution and analysis” and treats the official product plus learning materials as the playable package and gameplay/access evidence.
