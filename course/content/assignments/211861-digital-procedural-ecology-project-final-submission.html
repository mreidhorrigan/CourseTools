Submit one ZIP containing a complete browser-playable system and the evidence needed to assess its rules, variation, testing, and production. One team member may upload the ZIP; every member must test the submitted file.

Playable package

  • Place index.html at the ZIP root. The game must open in a current desktop browser through the documented method.
  • Use local relative paths for required assets. Include source files and only the dependencies needed to run or inspect the submission.
  • Implement a repeatable player loop with a clear action and at least two interacting entities, resources, or environmental processes.
  • Make state and feedback legible enough that a player can connect actions and system changes.
  • Provide instructions, keyboard access where controls permit it, readable text and contrast, and alternatives for essential information conveyed only by colour, sound, or animation.

Documentation and evidence

  • README: give launch instructions, supported browser, controls, player goal, known limitations, and a concise explanation of the procedural ecosystem.
  • Reproduction record: provide a seed, initial-state description, saved configuration, or exact action sequence that allows a marker to reproduce at least one reported outcome.
  • Gameplay video: show a complete representative interaction, visible feedback, and an emergent or variable outcome.
  • External test and revision record: identify test date and participant roles; record observations separately from interpretations; connect at least one observed problem to a revision or evidence-based retention decision.
  • Credits and licences: identify creators, sources, licences, and modifications for third-party code and assets. State which work is original.
  • Individual contribution evidence: for each member, list concrete contributions and analyze one effect on the integrated system.
  • AI appendix, when applicable: include complete relevant chatlogs when AI contributed to the submitted work. Remove credentials and private third-party information. No appendix is required when AI was not used.

Evaluation focus

A procedural ecosystem is a system in which multiple stateful elements affect one another over time. A strong emergent outcome follows from interacting rules and conditions, varies when relevant inputs change, and can be observed or reproduced; a scripted sequence alone does not demonstrate emergence. A small integrated system can satisfy the assignment more successfully than a large collection of disconnected or broken features.
