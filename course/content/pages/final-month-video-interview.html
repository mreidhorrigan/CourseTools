Required Final-Month Video Interview

Each student must schedule and attend one synchronous video interview with the instructor or a teaching assistant during the final month of the course. Appointments must be selected from the availability times posted by the instructional team. The meeting may address project process, individual contribution, course concepts, or final-exam preparation. The interview is required but is not separately graded. A student who cannot use any posted time must contact the instructional team before the booking window closes.

What to do

  1. Use the scheduling link or sign-up method posted by the instructor or TA.
  2. Select one of the provided availability times during the final month of the course.
  3. Attend the synchronous video meeting and be ready to discuss your project process, contribution, course concepts, or final-exam preparation.

Scheduling links and exact availability will be added here when booking opens.

This requirement is not separately graded. Contact the instructional team before the booking window closes if none of the posted times is possible.
